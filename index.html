SEW / RESEARCH
Research software · early prototype

A proposed framework for evaluating candidate worlds

SEW links candidate environment inputs to three research questions: how adaptive search may emerge, how chemical anomalies can be measured, and how evidence compares natural and technological explanations. The current site presents the framework and development status. It does not run the full Paper I, II, or III calculations.
