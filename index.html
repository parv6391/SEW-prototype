SEW / RESEARCH
Research software · early prototype

From environment inputs to research questions.

SEW is a project for exploring generated environments through a connected research framework.

Scenario generator

Random values are generated automatically.
Illustrative environment
Each scenario is fictional and can be replayed from its seed.
Current limits: The ranges are placeholders, not observations or calibrated distributions. Temperature is an equilibrium estimate, not surface climate. This demo does not calculate habitability, CAI, CIP, or ETI likelihood.

Research framework

PAPER I · ADAPTIVE SEARCH

Relates information, usable energy, and entropy to adaptive technological activity.

Not implemented in this demo

PAPER II · CAI

Measures chemical anomaly. Use requires chemical observables and a natural reference baseline.

Baseline needed

PAPER III · CIP

Compares explanations. A numerical result requires calibrated likelihoods and priors.

Calibration needed

No CAI, CIP, habitability, or ETI likelihood is calculated here.
