SEW / RESEARCH
Research software · early prototype

A proposed framework for evaluating candidate worlds

SEW connects environmental inputs to questions about adaptive search, chemical anomalies, and how strongly evidence favors different explanations. This page describes the research pathway and what the current demo does. The full Paper I to III calculations are not implemented here.
